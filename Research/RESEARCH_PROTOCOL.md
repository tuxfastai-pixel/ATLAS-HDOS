# Research Protocol

## Purpose

Research informs product direction, architecture, AI behavior, learning models, and user experience.

## Standards

- Record sources, assumptions, and open questions.
- Distinguish evidence from interpretation.
- Tie research findings to product decisions when possible.
- Do not treat speculation as a requirement.
- Revisit research when major product assumptions change.

## Research Note Template

```text
Topic:
Question:
Source or evidence:
Key finding:
Product implication:
Confidence:
Open questions:
```
