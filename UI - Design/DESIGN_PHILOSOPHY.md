# Design Philosophy

Atlas HDOS should feel calm, structured, and empowering.

## Principles

- Prioritize clarity over decoration.
- Make progress visible.
- Keep workflows focused and humane.
- Design for repeated use.
- Use accessible colors, typography, spacing, and interaction states.
- Prefer the PSG Design System when it becomes available.

## Product Feel

Atlas should help users understand where they are, what matters next, and how to move forward. The interface should support serious work without feeling cold or mechanical.
