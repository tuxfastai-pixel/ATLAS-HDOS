# AI Rules

## Product Principles

- AI must support the Atlas mission.
- AI must preserve user agency.
- AI behavior must be explainable in product terms.
- AI features need explicit acceptance criteria.

## Safety Rules

- Do not invent user facts.
- Do not make unsupported legal, medical, financial, or credential claims.
- Treat model output as untrusted until validated.
- Require explicit approval before destructive or irreversible actions.

## Engineering Rules

- Document prompts, tools, retrieval sources, and model boundaries.
- Keep AI-specific evaluation examples close to the feature they validate.
- Log AI errors and failures in a privacy-aware way.
- Keep sensitive data out of AI calls unless the flow is approved.
